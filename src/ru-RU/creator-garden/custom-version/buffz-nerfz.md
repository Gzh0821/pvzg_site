**PAD Industrial Ltd.'s extensive rebalance mod for Gardendless that rebalances a plethora plants in a way that each plant is a viable pick in the seed selection.**

## Features

- Thoroughly rebalanced plants to ensure they all of their own strengths and weaknesses, with many rebalances aiming to rework plants to be true to vanilla
- More unlockable adventure plants in every world, with each and every one of them having their own demonstration level!
- Numerous reworked and rebalanced levels (especially the expansion Zomboss fights) to suit balancing changes, as well as some new ones, such as a completely overhauled Dark Ages expansion containing new plants, fog, galore!
- Tiering for certain plants, each tier having alternate rebalances or completely new gimmicks to accommodate certain playstyles
- Several new QoL changes such as ticket/gem conversions, re-playable dialogue, new premium locations, etc.
- A comprehensive and reorganized almanac including a lot more stats such as Plant Food effects, production rates, fire rates, splash damage, etc.

## Note

- Make sure that "Dynamic Plant Registry", "Shop Extensions", and "plant-levels.json" are all enabled in the F9 menu. These can be found in the "Settings" and "Experimental" tabs respectively.
- Please read the changelog file inside the download for installation steps (*certain rebalances in the changelog may be outdated, if so, refer to the almanac in-game).
- For more info, you can ask me in my mod forum in the GE Discord server.
